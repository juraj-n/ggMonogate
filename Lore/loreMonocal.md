## 1. Biological Impact & Mindset

* Average lifespan runs 90–120 years. Their hearing and sense of smell are sharp, attuned to detecting the subtle shifts of the jungle — a snapped twig, a change in bird-call, the smell of rain before it falls.
*   **Psychological Effect:** Because their bodies are built for climbing, gripping, and enduring rather than striking first, Monocals instinctively think in terms of stability and patience rather than speed of aggression. Time is measured less by clocks and more by the rhythms of the jungle — the growth of a vine, the turning of seasons, the age of a tree. This gives most Monocals a long view of conflict: they are rarely provoked into rash action, preferring to outlast a threat rather than meet it head-on. Even when a fight becomes unavoidable, a Monocal's instinct is not to kill but to end the threat — a well-placed strike to disarm, a hold to immobilize, a blow calculated to knock an opponent unconscious rather than open their skin.
